Update a tinyfota board

Over Bluetooth with no cable, or down a USB cable. The board is identified first, so you find out what applies to it before you press anything on it. Nothing is written until you open the update window on the board itself.

Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Chrome on Android: Web Bluetooth and WebSerial are not in Safari or Firefox.

Checking what is published…

The release index is ota/ota-index.json beside this page, as tinyfota release --out-dir ota writes it. A board running an image no published update starts from has no over-the-air path; with a whole image published, reinstall everything writes it through MCUboot's serial recovery instead.
